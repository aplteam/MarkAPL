A paragraph can contain bold, italic, bold and italic, strike-through, inline code, an abbr with a tooltip, water H2O, the nth item, and syntactical sugar such as an arrow → and a copyright ©. Long words break: {{⍵/⍨2=+⌿0=⍵∘.|⍵}⍳⍵}.
